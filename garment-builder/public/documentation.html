🎯 Design Process

Creating Your First Design

1. Select Garment Type

Choose from our comprehensive garment library:

  • Kurti: Traditional and contemporary styles
  • Lehenga: Bridal, party, and casual variations
  • Saree Blouse: Classic and modern cuts
  • Anarkali: Floor-length and knee-length options

2. Configure Basic Settings

  • Neckline: Round, V-neck, boat neck, high neck
  • Sleeves: Full sleeve, half sleeve, sleeveless, cap sleeves
  • Hem Style: Straight, A-line, flared, asymmetrical
  • Fit: Regular, slim, loose, tailored

3. Apply Fabrics

Drag fabrics from the right panel onto different garment parts:

  • Main body fabric
  • Contrast panels
  • Lining materials
  • Embellishment areas

4. Add Embellishments

Enhance your design with:

  • Embroidery: Traditional and contemporary patterns
  • Beadwork: Sequins, pearls, crystals
  • Lace & Trims: Borders, appliques, ribbons
  • Prints: Block prints, digital patterns

💡 Pro Tip

Use the preview feature frequently to see how your changes affect the overall design. The real-time rendering helps you make informed decisions about fabric combinations and embellishment placement.

Custom Upload

Upload your own fabric images (JPG, PNG) up to 5MB. Ensure high resolution (1024x1024px) for best results.

⚠️ Fabric Selection Tips

  • Consider the garment's intended use and season
  • Match fabric weight with design complexity
  • Test color combinations using the preview feature
  • Account for fabric drape and structure

Custom Fabric Upload

To upload your own fabrics:

  1. Click "Upload Custom Fabric" in the fabric panel
  2. Select high-resolution images (recommended: 1024x1024px)
  3. Add fabric details: name, type, care instructions
  4. Save to your personal fabric library

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

Fabric Not Applying to Garment

Solution: Ensure you're dragging the fabric onto the correct garment part. Look for the blue highlight indicating a valid drop zone.

Design Not Saving

Solution: Check your internet connection and ensure you're logged in. The system auto-saves every 30 seconds when connected.

Preview Not Loading

Solution: Refresh your browser and clear cache. Ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

Custom Fabric Upload Failed

Solution: Verify image size (max 5MB) and format (JPG, PNG). Reduce file size if necessary using image compression tools.

System Requirements

  • Browser: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
  • Internet: Stable broadband connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended)
  • Display: 1280x720 minimum resolution (1920x1080 recommended)
  • Memory: 4GB RAM minimum (8GB recommended for complex designs)
